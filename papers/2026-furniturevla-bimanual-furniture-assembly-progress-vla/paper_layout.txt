Resolution Upscaling. To retain fine-grained visual cues
for contact-rich bimanual assembly, we increase the input
resolution of the PaliGemma–SigLIP vision backbone from its
pretrained 224×224 to 448×448. The target resolution aligns
with the SigLIP So400m/14 patch size, yielding a 32×32
token grid (448/14 = 32) without truncation. Input images
are resized with aspect-ratio-preserving interpolation and zero-
padded to 448×448. The pretrained positional embeddings
(24×24) are resized to match the new 32×32 token grid via
bicubic interpolation. All other pretrained parameters are kept
unchanged, and the backbone is finetuned end-to-end with
the action expert.
